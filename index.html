NYC Architecture Biennial

Sheet B-100

As President of the NYC Architecture Biennial (NYCAB), I lead the strategic vision, growth, and operations of an international nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing architecture, urban design, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Founded as a 501(c)(3) educational organization, NYCAB brings together architects, designers, academics, public officials, and industry leaders to foster dialogue on the future of the built environment through conferences, exhibitions, awards, educational programs, and digital initiatives.

About

President, NYC Architecture Biennial

I oversee all organizational, creative, and operational aspects of the Biennial, establishing annual themes, developing partnerships, securing sponsorships, managing budgets, and directing multidisciplinary teams of volunteers, consultants, and collaborators. Working closely with architects, universities, professional organizations, technology companies, and public institutions, I cultivate strategic relationships that expand the organization's impact and international reach.

My responsibilities include developing conference programming, curating keynote speakers and panel discussions, coordinating continuing education initiatives, supervising branding and marketing strategies, directing website development and digital communications, and managing event logistics from concept through execution. I also lead fundraising efforts, oversee the organization's governance, and guide long-term planning to support NYCAB's educational mission and financial sustainability.

Under my leadership, the Biennial has expanded its international presence by promoting cross-cultural dialogue and interdisciplinary collaboration at the intersection of architecture, technology, sustainability, and society. Signature initiatives include the annual international conference, the iN Awards recognizing innovation in architecture and design, online educational programs in collaboration with Pertanto, and partnerships that connect professionals across the Americas and beyond.

This role combines executive leadership, strategic planning, business development, event production, communications, stakeholder engagement, and architectural advocacy, reflecting my commitment to advancing design excellence while creating opportunities for education, professional exchange, and innovation within the global architectural community.
